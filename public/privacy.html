Privacy Policy

How Qiansi Sourcing (Kunming Zhoubiao Trading Co., Ltd., "we", "us") collects, uses, transfers and protects your personal information — and the rights you have over it. Last updated: 2026-08-23.

1. Who we are

Qiansi Sourcing is operated by Kunming Zhoubiao Trading Co., Ltd., registered in Kunming, Yunnan, China. We help importers and wholesalers source goods from Chinese factories and match supply with demand.

You can contact us about privacy at: zhoubiao@zhoubiaotrade.cn

2. What personal information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. We collect two groups:

  • Inquiry form ("Send Inquiry"): your name, your WhatsApp number or e-mail, and the description of what you need.
  • Supply & Demand wall ("Publish"): the product/item name, category, reference price, MOQ/quantity, delivery time, target market/destination, your contact details, a detailed description, and optionally a product image.

We ask you not to upload images containing other people's personal information, faces, vehicle license plates, addresses or similar sensitive details. Please only share an image of the product itself.

3. Why we process it (purpose and lawful basis)

We use this information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide a price and shipping quote;
  • To match you with suitable Chinese factories or with the other side of a supply/demand posting;
  • To publish your supply/demand post on the public wall and connect the two sides.

Our lawful bases are your consent (which you give when you submit a form, as stated above the submit button) and performance of a service you have requested.

4. Who processes it

Your information is processed by:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) — a third-party e-mail form processor. When you submit the inquiry form, it forwards your message to our inbox.
  • Our supply-and-demand wall Worker ("qiansi-sd-wall"), hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which stores and displays public supply/demand posts and the images you upload.

These are data processors acting on our behalf.

5. Cross-border transfer

Qiansi Sourcing is based in China. The data processors above are located or host servers outside your country of residence. This means that the personal information you submit may be transferred across borders and processed on servers located in other jurisdictions (for example, in the region where Cloudflare's or FormSubmit's infrastructure is located) to complete your request. By submitting a form, you understand and agree that your information will be transferred and processed outside your country of residence for the purposes described above.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your personal information only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Inquiry details: kept to respond to you and manage our ongoing relationship, and deleted when no longer needed for that purpose.
  • Supply & demand posts: kept for as long as the post remains publicly displayed on the wall, and removed when you (or we) delete it, or when it is no longer relevant.

We do not keep personal information longer than necessary.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or China's Personal Information Protection Law), you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • Ask us to delete your personal information;
  • Withdraw your consent at any time;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zhoubiao@zhoubiaotrade.cn. We will respond as required by applicable law. If you have a public wall post you want removed, mention the post and we will take it down.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request, please e-mail us at zhoubiao@zhoubiaotrade.cn.
