# Qiansi Sourcing — for AI agents

> This website is built to be consumed by AI agents as well as human visitors. Everything below is a pointer to machine-readable material the platform publishes for agent self-service. Humans can browse the normal site; agents should read the files listed here.

## What this is

Qiansi Sourcing (Kunming Zhoubiao Trading Co., Ltd.) is a China sourcing platform serving importers and wholesalers worldwide with factory-direct supply from verified Chinese factories. Services: factory wholesale prices, pre-shipment quality inspection, small parcel & dedicated-line logistics. Chinese, English and Portuguese supported.

   The single machine-readable authoritative source for facts, prices, MOQs, logistics, service fee, security/AI-safety rules and contact channels. Read this first.
