Importing from China to Saudi Arabia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-21, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

What was the China–Saudi Arabia bilateral trade volume in 2025?

US$108.16 billion in 2025 (China's exports: $53.42bn; imports from Saudi: $54.74bn). In Q1 2026 the two-way trade reached $24.15bn. Source: China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs country page, updated July 2026.

What share of Saudi Arabia's imports come from China?

27.5% in 2025, making China the #1 import source (US 8.2%, UAE 5.7% behind). Source: MofCom Economic & Commercial Office in KSA / GASTAT, published 2026-07-10.

What does Saudi Arabia import most?

Machinery and electronic products rank first, followed by transport equipment, base metals, chemicals and minerals. Saudi goods imports totaled about US$253.3bn in 2025, up 8.8% year-on-year. Source: MofCom Economic & Commercial Office in KSA / GASTAT, published 2026-07-10.

How connected is Saudi Arabia's population?

33.9 million internet users (99.0% penetration) and 34.1 million social media identities (99.6% of population) in January 2025; median age 29.6; urbanization 85.3%. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Saudi Arabia, January 2025.

What is Saudi Arabia's GDP and GDP per capita?

GDP ≈ US$1.277 trillion in 2025 (real growth 4.5%); nominal GDP per capita ≈ US$25,066. Sources: TradingEconomics / World Bank, 2026-08; MofCom KSA 2026-07-10 for growth.

Is there a free trade agreement between China and the GCC?

Not yet signed: negotiations are ongoing — the 11th round was held in Guangzhou on 21 October 2024, and China's MofCom said in May 2025 it is working to conclude the talks soon. Source: China FTA Network (fta.mofcom.gov.cn), fetched 2026-08-21.
