Importing from China to Thailand — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

⚠ Important: Thai customs rules changed on 2026-01-01

Thailand now levies import duty plus VAT on all goods valued 1 THB or more — the low-value duty-free exemption is gone — and the FCL container inspection rate was raised to 30%. Plan duty and VAT into your landed cost; there is no low-price direct-mail shortcut. Source: MOFCOM Economic and Commercial Office in Thailand, 2026-08 report.

Is China really Thailand's biggest trading partner?

Yes. In 2025, China–Thailand bilateral trade reached USD 153.26 billion, up 14.4% year-on-year; China's exports to Thailand grew 20.3% to USD 103.5 billion. China is both Thailand's largest trading partner and its largest FTA partner — in the first half of 2026, China–Thailand trade accounted for 37.6% of Thailand's trade with all FTA partners. Sources: Ministry of Foreign Affairs country profile, updated 2026-04; MOFCOM Thailand office.

What changed in Thai customs rules on 1 January 2026?

From 2026-01-01, Thai customs levies import duty plus VAT on all goods valued at 1 THB or more — the low-value duty-free exemption is abolished — and the physical inspection rate for full-container-load (FCL) shipments was raised to 30%. Source: MOFCOM Economic and Commercial Office in Thailand, 2026-08 report.

Is there still a low-cost direct-mail way to sell into Thailand?

No. Since 2026-01-01 there is no low-value duty-free channel: every shipment valued 1 THB or above is subject to import duty and VAT, and full-container shipments face a 30% inspection rate, so low-price direct-mail models now carry higher cost and less predictable clearance times. Duty and VAT must be planned into the landed cost, and sellers must meet Thailand's ETDA disclosure rules for platforms (effective 2025-12-31). Sources: MOFCOM Economic and Commercial Office in Thailand, 2026-08 report.

How digitally mature is Thailand as a market?

Thailand is the most digitally mature of the main Southeast Asian markets: internet penetration is 91% (2024), LINE reaches 78.2% of the population, and the median age is 40.6. Sources: World Bank (2024); DataReportal Digital 2025: Thailand (January 2025).

How large are Thailand's imports and where does China rank?

Thailand's total imports were USD 342.97 billion in 2025, with China as the second-largest source at about 15% (Japan 20%, EU 8%). Top import categories in 2025: machinery and electronics USD 89.51 billion, mineral fuels USD 42.14 billion, machinery USD 39.96 billion. Source: TradingEconomics, June 2026 update.

Are there tariff advantages for China–Thailand trade?

Yes. Since October 2003, vegetables and fruits have been duty-free between China and Thailand under the China–ASEAN FTA (ACFTA), and ACFTA plus RCEP preferences run in parallel for other categories — check the specific HS code rate before ordering. Trade settlement is also facilitated by the RMB clearing arrangement and the bilateral local-currency swap renewed in August 2025. Sources: Ministry of Foreign Affairs country profile, updated 2026-04.
