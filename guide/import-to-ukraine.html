Importing from China to Ukraine — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Ukraine trade?

China's exports to Ukraine reached USD 3.70 billion in 2024 (3,703,502,972, estimated series, China-side FOB). This is an estimated figure and should be confirmed against official Ukrainian import statistics. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, period=2024, flowCode=X, partnerCode=804), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Ukraine?

Ukraine is not a member of the EAEU, and there is no comprehensive China–Ukraine free-trade agreement, so Ukraine's own customs duty schedule applies to imported goods. Ukraine's tariff treatment is shaped by the EU Deep and Comprehensive Free Trade Area (DCFTA) framework — signed 2014, provisionally applied from 2016-01-01 and fully in force from 2017-09-01. Exact duty rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release. Sources: EU trade policy for Ukraine (policy.trade.ec.europa.eu), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Ukraine?

Ukraine's standard value-added tax rate is 20%, applied at customs on imported goods. Reduced or exempt treatment can apply to certain categories. Specific rates must be verified against the relevant Ukraine customs / tax authority — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release. Source: public tax-rate reference (PwC Tax Summaries), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Ukraine?

The State Enterprise for Standardization, Certification and Quality (SE UkrNDNC) and related bodies oversee mandatory certification and conformity in Ukraine. The relevant authority's site was unreachable this session, so the certification regime is presented as institution context only and category-specific requirements must be confirmed with the competent authority before import. Treat any figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release. Source: SE UkrNDNC / Ukraine standards — site blocked this session (Data gap).

What does Ukraine import from China?

Ukraine's main imports from China (indicative, incomplete, per-chapter) include electronics and electrical goods (HS85, roughly USD 802 million), optical and precision instruments (HS90, ~USD 120 million), toys and sports goods (HS95, ~USD 90 million), furniture and bedding (HS94, ~USD 74 million), prepared fish and meat (HS16, ~USD 57 million), glass (HS70, ~USD 25 million) and fertilizers (HS31, ~USD 13 million). WARNING: the captured chapter sum (~USD 1.21B) is far below the reported total (USD 3.70B) due to free-tier truncation — treat this profile as incomplete/indicative, not a full picture. Source: UN Comtrade (reporter=156, per-chapter), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to Ukraine?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or CMR (road/rail consignment note), and a certificate of origin to claim any applicable preferences. Ukraine's main gateways are the Black Sea ports of Odesa, Chornomorsk and Pivdennyi; wartime alternate routes via Poland / Romania / the Danube (EU Solidarity Lanes) are also used. Specific route details could not be independently verified this session — treat routing as established background and re-confirm at order time.
