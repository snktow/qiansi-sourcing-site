Importing from China to Russia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from real, traceable sources with the source and date named. Items that are category-specific or move over time are marked "to be verified" and must be re-checked with Russian customs / the EAEU body before you order.

How big is China–Russia trade?

Chinese exports to Russia reached USD 115.28 billion in 2024, per the UN COMTRADE database as aggregated by TradingEconomics (page last updated 2026-08). The same database shows Russia's imports-from-China series lagging at USD 72.69B for 2021. Russia's total monthly imports were about USD 28.7 billion in May 2026 and exports about USD 43.3 billion in June 2026. Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE.

What does China export to Russia?

China's exports to Russia are concentrated in machinery and equipment, electronics and consumer electronics, vehicles and auto parts, textiles, footwear and consumer goods. Certain dual-use items are subject to China's export-control regime — confirm the current list before shipping. Source: standard trade composition; export-control note to be verified against current Chinese regulations.

What tariff framework applies to imports from China?

Russia is part of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU). Goods from China — a non-EAEU third country — are charged under the EAEU Common Customs Tariff (ЕТТ ЕАЭС) set out in the TN VED schedule. There is no China–Russia free-trade agreement, so Chinese goods get no preference. Rates span a wide range by HS code; confirm the exact rate with the Russian Federal Customs Service. Source: EAEU CCT / TN VED.

What is Russia's VAT and are there recycling fees?

Russia's standard VAT is 22% effective 1 January 2026 (raised from 20%), per the TradingEconomics sales-tax-rate page (retrieved 2026-08). Confirm with the Russian Federal Tax Service. Some durable categories, notably certain cars and machinery, may also carry a utilization (recycling) fee — marked "to be verified" per current HS code.

What certification does Russia require?

Many goods need EAC / TR CU conformity certification and marking under EAEU technical regulations before they can be sold or imported. This commonly affects electronics, machinery, toys, appliances and protective goods. Requirements are product-specific — verify the applicable EAEU technical regulation (TR CU) for your HS code.

How do goods ship from China to Russia?

The dominant route is the China–Europe rail corridor: trains from Chongqing, Chengdu, Xi'an and other hubs cross at Manzhouli, Zabaikalsk or Suifenhe and run to Moscow and St. Petersburg, typically 15–25 days. Sea freight via Vladivostok and land transshipment are alternatives. From Kunming (Yunnan), goods consolidate to the rail hubs. Exact transit and rates depend on the shipment — request a forwarder quote for your route.
