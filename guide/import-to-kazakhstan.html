Importing from China to Kazakhstan — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

What was the total trade volume between China and Kazakhstan in 2025?

USD 48.7 billion, up 11% year-on-year — China's exports to Kazakhstan USD 29.7 billion (+6.2%) and imports from Kazakhstan USD 19 billion (+19.3%). Source: China MFA, "China-Kazakhstan Relations" (page updated 2026-06; fetched 2026-08-23).

How large is China's share of Kazakhstan's imports?

China was Kazakhstan's 2nd-largest import source in 2024 with USD 15.15 billion (≈25.3% of total imports of USD 59.79 billion), behind Russia (USD 18.25 billion, ≈30.5%). Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2024 (fetched 2026-08-23; shares are computed).

How big is the Kazakhstan market?

Population ≈20.5–20.8 million (2025–2026), GDP ≈USD 306 billion (2025, +6.5%), GDP per capita ≈USD 14,700 (2025) — a small but relatively high-income market in Central Asia. Source: World Bank / TradingEconomics (2026-08); China MFA country profile (updated 2026-06).

How digitally connected is Kazakhstan?

19.2 million internet users (92.9% penetration) and 15.7 million social media users (75.7% of population) as of January 2025; median age 29.7; urbanization 58.5%. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Kazakhstan (fetched 2026-08-23).

What import tariffs apply to Chinese goods in Kazakhstan?

No China-Kazakhstan FTA — the EAEU common external tariff applies (Kazakhstan joined the EAEU Customs Union framework in 2010/2014). MFN average applied tariff ≈5.6% (2023); WTO-bound caps 6.2% for industrial and 18.2% for agricultural goods. The China-EAEU trade & economic cooperation agreement (in force Oct 2019) is a cooperation framework, not a tariff-cutting FTA. RCEP does not apply. Source: trade.gov Kazakhstan Country Commercial Guide (published 2026-03-10; fetched 2026-08-23).

What were Kazakhstan's top import categories in 2024?

Machinery USD 9.54 billion (≈16%), vehicles (non-railway) USD 6.43 billion (≈10.8%), electrical & electronic equipment USD 5.38 billion (≈9%), out of total imports of USD 59.79 billion. Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2024 (fetched 2026-08-23; shares are computed).
