Importing from China to Bulgaria — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Bulgaria trade?

China's exports to Bulgaria reached an estimated USD 2.83 billion in 2024 (2,834,857,025, China-side FOB). UN Comtrade flags this value as ESTIMATED (legacyEstimationFlag=4, isReported=false) — an estimate, not a confirmed customs figure. Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so a leaf-only sum (~USD 144.7M) or full row-sum (~USD 434.1M) is only a partial subset, NOT the total. Confirm against official China/Bulgaria statistics before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, partnerCode=100, period=2024, flowCode=X), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Bulgaria?

Bulgaria is an EU member state since 1 January 2007, so goods from China (a non-EU country) are subject to the EU Common Customs Tariff (CET / TARIC) MFN third-country rate. Bulgaria has been a WTO member since 1 December 1996. There is NO comprehensive EU–China free trade agreement, so Chinese goods generally pay the standard MFN duty unless a specific preferential or trade-defence measure applies. Exact duty rates depend on the HS code — confirm against the latest EU TARIC/official release; treat any single figure as indicative. Sources: WTO (Bulgaria), EU trade policy (China), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Bulgaria?

Bulgaria's standard value-added tax (VAT) rate is 20%. Confirmed by PwC Tax Summaries (Bulgaria) and TradingEconomics, which sources it to the Bulgarian tax/administration. Reduced rates can apply to specific categories — verify the current standard and reduced rates with the competent authority before import. Source: PwC Tax Summaries; TradingEconomics (Bulgaria Sales Tax Rate), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Bulgaria?

As an EU member, Bulgaria applies EU product rules. Products within the scope of EU harmonised legislation must bear the CE marking, confirming the manufacturer declares the product meets EU product requirements and allowing free marketing across the EU regardless of origin. Importers/authorised representatives bear EU compliance responsibility. Data gap: the Bulgarian customs administration site was unreachable this session, so the itemised local declaration/notified-body list could not be verified from an official primary source — confirm with the competent authority before import. Sources: EU Your Europe (CE marking), retrieved 2026-08-23; Bulgarian customs = Data gap.

What does Bulgaria import from China?

Bulgaria's main imports from China (indicative and incomplete, per the 500-row free preview) include semi-milled/wholly milled rice (HS100630, ~USD 9.69M), nicotine-bearing inhalation e-vapour products (HS240412, ~USD 8.84M), heterocyclic compounds (HS293399, ~USD 7.58M), other spirits/liqueurs (HS220890, ~USD 7.54M) and carob/edible plant products (HS121299, ~USD 7.51M). CAUTION: these are a truncated, mixed-level preview subset and very likely NOT the true top categories — indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Bulgaria?

Varna is Bulgaria's primary Black Sea port and northern gateway, operated by the Port of Varna (port-varna.bg). Burgas is the major southern Black Sea commercial port and Ruse is the Danube river gateway to Central Europe — both are stable geographic facts, but their operator official sites were unreachable this session (Data gap). As an EU member, cargo landed at Varna/Burgas moves inland by road/rail into Central Europe, or arrives via the China–Europe rail corridor. Sources: Port of Varna, retrieved 2026-08-23; Burgas/Ruse operators = Data gap.
