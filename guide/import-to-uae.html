Importing from China to the UAE — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from real, traceable sources with the source and date named. Category-specific duties and certifications must be re-checked with UAE Customs and the relevant body before you order.

How big is China–UAE trade?

Chinese exports to the UAE reached USD 65.59 billion in 2024; the UAE's imports-from-China figure in the same UN COMTRADE database was USD 83.98 billion for 2024 — the gap reflects different valuation and re-export accounting. Both are from TradingEconomics (page last updated 2026-08). The UAE is China's largest trading partner in the Arab world, and Jebel Ali is a major re-export hub. This is an estimated series from UN Comtrade (not a directly reported figure); Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so any per-row sum is only a partial subset, NOT the total — the headline above is the cmdCode=TOTAL estimate. Confirm against the destination country's official import statistics before quoting. Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE aggregate (cmdCode=TOTAL, ESTIMATED), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does China export to the UAE?

China's exports to the UAE include electronics and telecom equipment, machinery, textiles and apparel, construction materials (steel, aluminium, ceramics, glass), auto parts and consumer goods, with renewable and energy-storage hardware growing rapidly.

What tariff applies to imports from China?

The UAE applies the GCC common external tariff. Most general goods carry about 5% (roughly 0–5% range), with some foodstuffs and raw materials duty-free and tobacco/alcohol higher. There is no in-force China–GCC FTA, so Chinese goods get no preference and are charged the standard GCC rate. Confirm the exact rate with UAE Customs for your HS code. Source: GCC Common Tariff.

What is the UAE's VAT?

UAE VAT was raised from 5% to 9% on 1 January 2023. Note that the TradingEconomics "sales tax rate" page still shows 5% (lagging); the current standard VAT is 9%. Always confirm with the UAE Federal Tax Authority. Source: UAE FTA / TradingEconomics (retrieved 2026-08).

Does a free zone change my duty and VAT?

Yes. Goods stored, traded or re-exported from a UAE free zone (Jebel Ali Free Zone, DMCC, etc.) are generally free of customs duty and VAT; duty and VAT only apply when goods enter the UAE mainland for local sale. This is a key tool for re-export distribution to the GCC, Africa and South Asia. Verify current rules with the free-zone authority and UAE Customs.

What certification does the UAE require?

The UAE (via the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, formerly ESMA) runs the ECAS conformity assessment scheme with EQM marking, plus UAE RoHS, energy/water-efficiency labelling, TRA approval for wireless and telecom devices, and Halal certification for food, cosmetics and personal care. Note: SASO is a Saudi, not UAE, requirement. Requirements are product-specific — confirm per HS code.

How do goods ship from China to the UAE?

Sea freight from China to Jebel Ali typically takes around 15–28 days, and air freight to Dubai (DXB) about 2–5 days. From Kunming (Yunnan) goods consolidate by air via hubs such as Guangzhou, Hong Kong, Doha or Dubai, or by sea via Guangzhou/Shenzhen. Exact transit and rates depend on the shipment — request a forwarder quote.
