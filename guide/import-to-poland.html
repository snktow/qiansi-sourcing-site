Importing from China to Poland — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

How large is China–Poland trade?

Poland's imports from China reached USD 65.17 billion in 2025, and China's exports to Poland were USD 40.05 billion in 2024. The two series differ because they come from different customs/statistical sources and reference years. Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE (Poland imports, 2025) and China customs export data (2024), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Poland?

Poland is an EU member, so goods entering Poland from China are subject to the EU's Common Customs Tariff (CET) plus EU anti-dumping and trade-defence measures where applicable. Because there is no EU–China FTA, the standard third-country (MFN) duty applies. Exact rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time.

What VAT and duties apply to imports into Poland?

Poland's standard VAT rate is 23%, with reduced rates (8% and 5%) applied to certain goods and foodstuffs. Customs duty depends on the HS code. Specific rates must be verified against the official EU TARIC / Polish customs schedule — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release.

How does the China-Europe Railway Express reach Poland?

Poland is the main EU entry hub for the China-Europe Railway Express, with the Małaszewicze terminal being the primary gateway. Trains run on routes such as Xi'an/Chongqing through Khorgos and Dostyk, then via Brest (Belarus) into Małaszewicze, Poland, before distributing across Europe. Typical rail transit is around 12–18 days depending on the origin and route — a faster alternative to sea freight.

What does China export to Poland?

China's main exports to Poland are machinery and equipment, electronics and electrical goods, textiles and apparel, furniture and home goods, and automotive components. Source: China customs export composition, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What documentation is needed to import from China to Poland?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or CIM railway consignment note, certificate of origin, and any CE marking or EU conformity declarations required by the product category. Imports into the EU typically require an EORI number for customs clearance and must comply with applicable EU product safety and labelling rules. Certification requirements are category-specific and should be confirmed for your product.
