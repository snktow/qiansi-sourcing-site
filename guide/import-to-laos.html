Importing from China to Laos — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

What was the total trade volume between China and Laos in 2025?

USD 9.82 billion, up 19.3% year-on-year — China's exports to Laos USD 4.32 billion (+17.6%) and imports from Laos USD 5.5 billion (+20.7%). China is Laos's largest investor and 2nd-largest trading partner. Source: China MFA, "China-Laos Relations" (page updated 2026-07; fetched 2026-08-23).

How large is China's share of Laos's imports?

China was Laos's 2nd-largest import source in 2023 with USD 2.11 billion (≈29.8% of total imports of USD 7.08 billion), behind Thailand (USD 2.95 billion, ≈41.7%). Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2023 (fetched 2026-08-23; shares are computed).

How big is the Laos market?

Population ≈7.87 million (2025), GDP ≈USD 18.3 billion (2025, +4.5% growth), GDP per capita ≈USD 2,325 (2025) — one of the smallest consumer markets in Southeast Asia, heavily rural (39.2% urbanized). Source: World Bank Lao PDR country page (fetched 2026-08-23).

How digitally connected is Laos?

4.97 million internet users (63.6% penetration) and 4.25 million social media users (54.3% of population) as of January 2025; mobile connections 6.78 million (86.7%); median age 24.9; urbanization 39.2%. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Laos (fetched 2026-08-23).

What import tariffs apply to Chinese goods in Laos?

Laos is an ASEAN member, so the China-ASEAN FTA (ACFTA) preferential tariff applies to Chinese goods (ACFTA has been upgraded to version 3.0); Laos is also an RCEP member (in force for Laos since January 2022). Exact per-HS rates were not found — verify with Lao Customs or via ACFTA/RCEP tariff schedules before shipping. Standard VAT is 10%. Source: Background knowledge + MOFCOM office in Laos homepage (ACFTA 3.0 article; fetched 2026-08-23); exact rates marked to be verified.

How does the China-Laos Railway affect freight?

The Kunming–Vientiane railway opened on 3 December 2021. By mid-2026 it had carried over 80 million tonnes of freight (over 18 million tonnes cross-border, 3,000+ product categories) and over 70 million passenger trips; a "one checkpoint, two inspections" border agreement was signed in September 2025 to speed up customs clearance. Source: China MFA, "China-Laos Relations" (page updated 2026-07; fetched 2026-08-23).
