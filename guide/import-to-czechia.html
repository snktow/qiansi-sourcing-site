Importing from China to Czechia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Czechia trade?

China's exports to Czechia reached an estimated USD 17.5 billion in 2024 (17,540,311,507, China-side FOB). UN Comtrade flags this TOTAL aggregate as ESTIMATED (legacyEstimationFlag=4, isReported=false). Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so a leaf-only sum (~USD 213.7M) or full row-sum (~USD 649.3M) is only a partial subset, NOT the total. Confirm against official China/Czechia statistics (China Customs / CZSO) before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, partnerCode=203, cmdCode=TOTAL), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Czechia?

Czechia is an EU member state since 1 May 2004 (WTO since 1 Jan 1995, GATT since 15 Apr 1993). Goods from China (a non-EU country) are subject to the EU Common Customs Tariff (CET / TARIC) third-country ordinary rate. There is NO comprehensive EU–China free trade agreement, so Chinese goods generally pay the standard MFN duty, and may be subject to EU trade-defence measures (anti-dumping/countervailing). Exact rates depend on the HS code — confirm against the latest EU TARIC/Access2Markets and Czech customs. Sources: WTO (Czech Republic), EU Access2Markets - Tariffs, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Czechia?

Czechia's standard value-added tax (VAT) rate is 21%. Confirmed by TradingEconomics (Czech Republic Sales Tax Rate = 21%). Czechia applies reduced VAT tiers to specific categories — the exact reduced rate figure was not independently sourced this session (Data gap), so confirm with the latest Czech Ministry of Finance / EU VAT rate official release. Source: TradingEconomics, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Czechia?

As an EU member state, Czechia applies EU product rules. Many products require the CE marking before sale in the EU — the manufacturer declares the product meets EU product requirements, allowing free circulation regardless of origin. Higher-risk products (medical devices, construction products, machinery) may need third-party conformity assessment by an EU Notified Body, plus technical documentation and an EU Declaration of Conformity. Imports enter the EU common customs territory under the Union Customs Code. Data gap: a category-specific import-document list was not verified from an official primary source this session — confirm with Czech Customs Administration / a customs broker; certain categories (chemicals, electronics) may also fall under REACH/RoHS/WEEE. Sources: EU Your Europe (CE marking), EU Customs Union, Customs Administration of the Czech Republic, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does Czechia import from China?

Czechia's main imports from China (indicative and incomplete, per the 500-row free preview) include nicotine-bearing e-vapour products (HS240412, ~USD 45.60M), retail dog/cat food (HS230910, ~USD 16.97M), other food preparations (HS210690, ~USD 14.27M), heterocyclic compounds (HS293399, ~USD 11.67M) and other vegetable saps/extracts (HS130219, ~USD 6.93M). CAUTION: these are a truncated, mixed-level preview subset and very likely NOT the true top categories — indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Czechia?

Czechia is a landlocked country, so goods reach it via neighbouring ports and road/rail intermodal routes. Main sea gateways (via rail/road): Hamburg and Bremerhaven (Germany), Rotterdam (Netherlands), Antwerp (Belgium), Koper (Slovenia, Adriatic) and Gdansk/Gdynia (Poland). The China–Europe Railway Express links Czechia via the Poland–Belarus border nodes (e.g. Malaszewicze/Brest) into the EU, with main rail freight hubs around Prague and the Ústí nad Labem / Lovosice area. These are stable logistics/geography facts from the EU TEN-T framework and Customs Union. Specific train schedules/freight rates were not sourced this session (Data gap) — confirm with the carrier/freight forwarder. Sources: EU TEN-T, EU Customs Union, retrieved 2026-08-23.
