Importing from China to North Macedonia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–North Macedonia trade?

China's exports to North Macedonia reached an estimated USD 316 million in 2024 (315,601,621, China-side FOB). UN Comtrade flags this TOTAL aggregate as ESTIMATED (legacyEstimationFlag=4, isReported=false). Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so a leaf-only sum (~USD 36.9M) or full row-sum (~USD 110.6M) is only a partial subset, NOT the total. Confirm against official China/North Macedonia statistics (China Customs / State Statistical Office) before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, partnerCode=807, cmdCode=TOTAL), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into North Macedonia?

North Macedonia is a WTO member since 4 April 2003. It is NOT an EU member (EU candidate country; Stabilisation and Association Agreement signed 2004), so it does NOT apply the EU Common Customs Tariff — it applies its own national tariff schedule (WTO-bound rates + national MFN), influenced by preferential arrangements under CEFTA and the SAA/EU. Goods from China generally pay the national MFN duty. Exact rates depend on the HS code — confirm against the latest official North Macedonian customs tariff publication. Sources: WTO (North Macedonia), European Commission / CEFTA Secretariat, retrieved 2026-08-23. Specific national tariff rates = Data gap this session.

What VAT applies to imports into North Macedonia?

North Macedonia's standard value-added tax (VAT) rate is 18%. Confirmed by PwC Tax Summaries (North Macedonia) and TradingEconomics (Macedonia Sales Tax Rate = 18%). A reduced rate for some goods/services exists, but the exact reduced-tier figure was not independently sourced this session (Data gap) — confirm with the latest Ministry of Finance / tax authority release. Source: PwC Tax Summaries; TradingEconomics, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into North Macedonia?

North Macedonia is not an EU member, so the EU CE marking is NOT mandatory (CE is an EU mechanism). Imported goods are generally subject to national standards and conformity-assessment requirements (e.g. ISO / national standardisation); certain categories (food, pharmaceuticals, machinery, chemicals) may need national certification or conformity assessment. Data gap: the official standardisation/conformity-authority document list was not verified from an official primary source this session — confirm category-specific requirements with North Macedonia Customs / the national standardisation authority (e.g. the Standardization Institute of the Republic of North Macedonia). Source: national framework (not verified this session = Data gap), advice is indicative.

What does North Macedonia import from China?

North Macedonia's main imports from China (indicative and incomplete, per the 500-row free preview) include other chemical products/preparations (HS382499, ~USD 5.54M), propellant powders (HS360100, ~USD 3.03M), other petroleum resins/polysulphides (HS391190, ~USD 2.86M), other plastic articles (HS392690, ~USD 2.52M) and new pneumatic rubber tyres for buses/trucks (HS401120, ~USD 2.00M). CAUTION: these are a truncated, mixed-level preview subset and very likely NOT the true top categories — indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to North Macedonia?

North Macedonia is a landlocked country bordering Greece, Albania, Bulgaria, Kosovo and Serbia, so goods reach it via neighbouring ports and road/rail intermodal routes. Main sea gateways (via transit): Piraeus/Thessaloniki (Greece, via the Mediterranean into the Balkan interior), Durrës (Albania, Adriatic gateway via road/rail) and Varna/Burgas (Bulgaria, via the Black Sea and Balkan land corridors). These are stable logistics/geography facts. Data gap: the national transport/customs-operator specifics, rail/road freight corridors and freight rates were not sourced this session — confirm schedules and rates directly with the carrier/freight forwarder.
