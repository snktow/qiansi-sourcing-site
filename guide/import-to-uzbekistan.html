Importing from China to Uzbekistan — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

What was the total trade volume between China and Uzbekistan in 2025?

USD 16.1 billion, up 17.9% year-on-year — China's exports to Uzbekistan USD 14.23 billion (+22.2%) and imports from Uzbekistan USD 1.87 billion (-6.9%). Source: China MFA, "China-Uzbekistan Relations" (page updated 2026-06; fetched 2026-08-23).

How large is China's share of Uzbekistan's imports?

China's exports to Uzbekistan (USD 14.23 billion, 2025) equal ≈30.1% of Uzbekistan's total imports (USD 47.35 billion, 2025). Share is a computed value from the two official MFA figures, not an official statistic. Source: China MFA bilateral page + MFA country profile (updated 2026-06; computed, fetched 2026-08-23).

How big is the Uzbekistan market?

Population ≈36.7–38.4 million (2025–2026; largest in Central Asia), GDP USD 147 billion (2025, +7.7%), GDP per capita USD 3,968 (2025) — a fast-growing but low-income, price-sensitive market. Source: China MFA country profile (updated 2026-06); World Bank / TradingEconomics (2025 data, fetched 2026-08-23).

How digitally connected is Uzbekistan?

32.7 million internet users (89.0% penetration) and 11.7 million social media users (31.7% of population) as of January 2025; median age 27.0; urbanization 50.7%. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Uzbekistan (fetched 2026-08-23).

What import tariffs and certification apply to Chinese goods in Uzbekistan?

There is no China-Uzbekistan FTA; Uzbekistan is not an RCEP member and not an EAEU member (observer status), so Chinese goods are subject to Uzbekistan's MFN rates (no official tariff schedule was retrievable in this research — verify product HS codes with a local customs broker). Imported goods on Uzbekistan's mandatory list require a conformity certificate under Uzstandard (national standardization & conformity body) before customs clearance (no official source retrieved — treat as general industry knowledge). Source: MFA / mofcom signals + background knowledge; labeled inferred/unverified (fetched 2026-08-23).

What is the USD/UZS exchange rate and how is it moving?

USD/UZS ≈ 11,852 as of August 21, 2026 (the som strengthened ≈4.09% over the past 12 months); China MFA's country profile cites ≈12,000 som per USD (updated 2026-06). Source: TradingEconomics (fetched 2026-08-23); China MFA country profile (2026-06).
