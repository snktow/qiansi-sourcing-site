Importing from China to Pakistan — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

What was the total trade volume between China and Pakistan in 2024?

USD 23.06 billion, up 11.1% year-on-year — China's exports to Pakistan USD 20.23 billion (+17%) and imports from Pakistan USD 2.82 billion (-18.2%). China has been Pakistan's largest trading partner since the 2015/16 fiscal year (per Pakistani statistics). Source: China MFA, "China-Pakistan Relations" (page updated 2025-12; fetched 2026-08-23; data per China Customs).

How large is China's share of Pakistan's imports?

China was Pakistan's largest import source in 2025 with USD 20.49 billion out of total imports of USD 65.17 billion (≈31.4%, computed), followed by the UAE (USD 6.95 billion) and Indonesia (USD 4.34 billion). Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2025 (fetched 2026-08-23; share is computed).

How big is the Pakistan market?

Population ≈255.2 million (2025, World Bank), GDP ≈USD 407.3 billion (2025), GDP per capita ≈USD 1,596 (2025) — the world's 5th most populous country but a low-income, price-sensitive market. Source: World Bank / TradingEconomics (fetched 2026-08-23); China MFA trade page (2025-12).

How digitally connected is Pakistan?

116 million internet users (45.7% penetration) and 66.9 million social media user identities (26.4% of population) as of January 2025; median age 20.6; urbanization 38.6%. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Pakistan (fetched 2026-08-23).

What tariff preferences apply to Chinese goods in Pakistan?

The China-Pakistan FTA (CPFTA) entered into force in July 2007; the Phase-II protocol was signed in 2019 and took effect on 1 January 2020, phasing in tariff cuts across thousands of tariff lines. Specific duty rates must be verified by HS code with Pakistani customs/brokers (not retrieved here). Source: China MFA, "China-Pakistan Relations" (updated 2025-12) for agreement facts; specific rates not retrieved.

What is the USD/PKR exchange rate?

USD 1 ≈ PKR 277.6 as of 21 August 2026; the rupee strengthened 2.16% over the past 12 months (all-time high USD/PKR 307.75 in September 2023). Source: TradingEconomics Pakistan Rupee page (fetched 2026-08-23).
