This is a reference range based on current Brazilian retail prices, not a promise of a specific profit margin. Your actual margin depends on your pricing, market and volume.

High-voltage (220V) or low-voltage (5V) — which should I choose?

Choose by application:

  • 220V COB — plug-and-play at mains voltage, no transformer needed. Best for outdoor building and facade lighting (IP65).
  • 5V WS2812B — individually addressable RGB, compatible with apps and controllers. Best for decorative, backlighting and programmable projects, needs a controller and power supply.
  • Silicone linear (IP67) — flexible and cuttable, for interior channels and outdoor profiles.

Voltage, IP rating and controller options are all configurable — we match the exact spec to your market.

How long does shipping take?

Air freight takes 5–10 days and sea freight 15–25 days, depending on the model.

Both small parcels (door-to-door with tracking) and dedicated lines are available; shipments over 50 kg move at a lower per-kg cost.

What import duties and taxes apply?

Import duties and taxes depend on the HS code of each strip type and each shipment.

For Brazil, low-value parcels fall under the Remessa Conforme simplified taxation scheme. Always check the Receita Federal portal (and for Malaysia the Royal Malaysian Customs) with the HS code for authoritative figures before ordering — we also include duties and taxes in your full quote.
