Importing from China to Slovenia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Slovenia trade?

China's exports to Slovenia reached an estimated USD 6.13 billion in 2024 (6,125,633,463, China-side FOB). UN Comtrade flags this TOTAL aggregate as ESTIMATED (legacyEstimationFlag=4, isReported=false). Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so a leaf-only sum (~USD 388.9M) or full row-sum (~USD 1.17B) is only a partial subset, NOT the total. Confirm against official China/Slovenia statistics before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, partnerCode=705, cmdCode=TOTAL), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Slovenia?

Slovenia is an EU member state since 1 May 2004 (WTO since 30 July 1995, GATT since 30 Oct 1994). Goods from China (a non-EU country) use the EU Common Customs Tariff (CET / TARIC) third-country/MFN rate. There is NO comprehensive EU–China free trade agreement, so Chinese goods generally pay the standard MFN duty, and may be subject to EU trade-defence measures. Exact rates depend on the HS code — confirm against the latest EU TARIC and Slovenian customs. Sources: WTO (Slovenia), European Commission CCT/TARIC, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Slovenia?

Slovenia's standard value-added tax (VAT) rate is 22%. Confirmed by both PwC Tax Summaries (Standard VAT rate = 22%) and TradingEconomics (Slovenia Sales Tax Rate = 22%). Slovenia also applies reduced VAT rates — the exact reduced-tier figures were not independently sourced this session (Data gap), so confirm with the latest Slovenian tax law. Source: PwC Tax Summaries; TradingEconomics (updated 2026-07-31), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Slovenia?

As an EU member state, Slovenia applies EU product rules. The CE marking is required only for products covered by harmonised EU rules (toys, drones, electrical/electronic equipment, pressure equipment, machinery, medical devices, measuring instruments, etc.); the manufacturer declares conformity, allowing free circulation regardless of origin. There is no central EU body issuing a CE certificate — the manufacturer/importer/authorised representative completes the conformity assessment. Imports follow EU customs procedures (SAD declaration, import VAT + duty). Slovenia-specific customs documentation was not captured this session (Data gap — gov.si unreachable). Sources: EU Your Europe (CE marking), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does Slovenia import from China?

Slovenia's main imports from China (indicative and incomplete, per the 500-row free preview) include heterocyclic compounds (HS293399, ~USD 92.66M), dosed medicaments (HS300490, ~USD 40.79M), isocyanates (HS292910, ~USD 28.91M), other heterocyclic compounds (HS293329, ~USD 13.29M) and pyrimidine/piperazine-ring compounds (HS293359, ~USD 10.16M). CAUTION: these are a truncated, mixed-level preview subset and very likely NOT the true top categories — indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Slovenia?

The Port of Koper is Slovenia's main sea port and Adriatic gateway, and is the eastern terminus of the EU Baltic Sea–Adriatic Sea corridor. The Port of Trieste (Italy) is a major port on the same corridor's western branch. The Mediterranean corridor is upgrading the Koper–Divača rail double-track to connect Koper's hinterland. Koper operator throughput/berth details were not captured this session (Data gap). Sources: European Commission Mobility and Transport (Baltic–Adriatic / Mediterranean corridors), retrieved 2026-08-23.
