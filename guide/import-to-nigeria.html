Importing from China to Nigeria — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "data gap".

How large is China–Nigeria trade?

China's exports to Nigeria reached USD 18.90 billion in 2024 (fob). This value is reported by the UN Comtrade public preview and is part of its estimated series; treat it as an estimate and confirm against Nigeria's own official import statistics. Source: UN Comtrade / COMTRADE public preview (China exports, estimated series, 2024), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Nigeria?

Nigeria is a member of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) and applies the ECOWAS Common External Tariff plus its own national customs tariff schedule. There is no comprehensive China–Nigeria free-trade agreement, so Chinese-origin goods are generally assessed at the most-favoured-nation (MFN) rate under the applicable tariff schedule. Exact rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release.

What VAT applies to imports into Nigeria?

Nigeria's standard VAT rate is reported at 7.5%, applied at import alongside customs duty. Note that Nigeria's Tax Reform Act 2025 provides for a phased increase in VAT, so the effective rate should be confirmed with the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) or the official gazette before placing an order — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release. Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries / TradingEconomics (Nigeria sales tax rate), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Nigeria?

Many imported goods into Nigeria require SONCAP (Standards Organisation of Nigeria Conformity Assessment Programme), administered by the Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON). Certain regulated products — notably food, drugs, cosmetics and chemicals — additionally require NAFDAC registration. Certification requirements are category-specific and should be confirmed for your product with the relevant Nigerian authorities. Source: Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does China export to Nigeria?

Nigeria imports from China a large share of machinery and equipment, electronics and electrical goods, textiles and apparel, building materials (steel, tiles, cement) and vehicles/parts. Detailed commodity composition is described at a high level here rather than as specific values. Source: UN Comtrade / COMTRADE export composition (China exports), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to Nigeria?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and any SONCAP/NAFDAC conformity documentation required. Goods arrive mainly by sea through the Port of Lagos (the Apapa and Tin Can Island complexes) — the largest port group in West Africa — with sea freight from China typically around 30–40 days; air freight to Lagos or Abuja is faster for smaller or urgent consignments. Exact transit times depend on the origin and route and are re-confirmed at order time.
