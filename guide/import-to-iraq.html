Importing from China to Iraq — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Iraq trade?

China's exports to Iraq reached USD 1.81 billion (ESTIMATED) in 2024 (1,810,693,251, China-side FOB). This is a raw partial/lower-bound figure: the UN Comtrade public preview API caps each response at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate-level with 6-digit leaf rows, so a simple row-sum double-counts and understates the true total. Treat it as indicative/partial, NOT an authoritative total. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, period=2024, flowCode=X, partnerCode=368), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Iraq?

Iraq is a WTO observer in accession, not a full member, with tariffs governed by the Customs Tariff Law. Since 2018 it has raised tariffs on selected imports and cut import licences. EU–Iraq relations rest on the Partnership and Cooperation Agreement signed 2012-05-11 — a NON-preferential trade agreement (WTO-based), not an FTA. Iraq also signed a TIFA with the US that took effect in 2013, and has FTAs with several Arab states currently suspended. Exact rates depend on the HS code — confirm the latest official release. Sources: WTO, US ITA CCG (2021-11-02), EU, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT / sales tax applies to imports into Iraq?

Iraq does NOT have a unified VAT. Instead a category-based sales tax applies: tobacco/alcohol 300%, mobile top-up cards and internet 20%, travel tickets and cars 15%, with an additional 10% on luxury restaurants and hotels. There is therefore no single VAT percentage to quote — a single figure would be misleading. Verify the rate for your category first. Source: PwC Tax Summaries (Last reviewed 24 June 2026), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Iraq?

Iraqi imports are governed by the Customs Law No. 23 (1984), and Iraq's national standards body is COSQC (Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control). Notably, from October 2020 Iraq contracted TÜV Rheinland to conduct overseas factory inspections on certain imported manufactured consumer goods — exporters or agents must obtain a certificate of registration to receive an import licence. Commercial documents typically require consular authentication and handwritten signatures. The specific mandatory standards/conformity-assessed product list could not be confirmed from an official source this session — Data gap for that detail. Sources: US ITA CCG (2021-11-02), COSQC official site.

What does Iraq import from China?

Iraq's main imports from China (indicative, incomplete, per the 500-row preview, aggregated at mutually exclusive HS-2-digit chapters) include preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts (HS20, ~USD 200 million), oilseeds and oleaginous fruits (HS12, ~USD 145 million), organic chemicals (HS29, ~USD 87 million), edible fruit and nuts (HS08, ~USD 43 million) and sugars (HS17, ~USD 24 million). This is a partial/lower-bound picture, NOT the full import total. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Iraq?

Data gap: specific port/land-crossing names could not be verified from any reachable official or authoritative source this session (Iraqi port authorities, the US ITA country guide, UN Logistics Cluster). As background only (stable geographic/logistics facts, not officially verified), Umm Qasr is Iraq's main port, with Grand Faw (new port) and the Basra waterway as associated gateways.
