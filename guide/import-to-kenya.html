Importing from China to Kenya — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified" or flagged as estimates.

How large is China–Kenya trade?

China's exports to Kenya reached USD 8.58 billion in 2024 — an estimated series from UN Comtrade, flagged as estimated rather than a directly reported figure. Because the value is an estimate, confirm it against Kenya's own official import statistics before relying on it for business planning. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (China customs, exporter reporting, 2024), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Kenya?

Kenya is a member of the East African Community (EAC) and COMESA, and applies the EAC Common External Tariff. There is no China–Kenya free-trade agreement, so Chinese-origin goods are assessed at the Most Favoured Nation (MFN) rate under the applicable tariff schedule. Exact rates depend on the HS code and the current trade regime; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official EAC/Kenya tariff.

What VAT and duties apply to imports into Kenya?

Kenya's standard VAT rate is 16%. Customs duty depends on the HS code and any applicable exemptions under the EAC Common External Tariff. Specific rates must be verified against the Kenya Revenue Authority / EAC schedule — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release.

What certification is required to import goods into Kenya?

The Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) runs the Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVoC) scheme, under which regulated products must be conformity-assessed or verified before shipment to Kenya. Which products fall under PVoC is category-specific, so confirm whether your goods are in scope with KEBS or your appointed inspection agency. Source: Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS), kebs.org, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does China export to Kenya?

The bulk of China's exports to Kenya is machinery and equipment, electronics and electrical goods, textiles and apparel, vehicles and parts, and building materials — an indicative profile consistent with China's exports across African markets. The precise HS-code breakdown should be confirmed against official Kenyan customs statistics.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to Kenya?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and any KEBS PVoC conformity documentation that applies. Goods arrive mainly by sea through the Port of Mombasa, with the Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) linking Mombasa to Nairobi; Kenya also acts as a transit/entry hub for the wider East African region. Typical sea freight from China is around 30–40 days depending on the origin and route, re-confirmed at order time.
