Importing from China to Sri Lanka — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified" or flagged as estimates.

How large is China–Sri Lanka trade?

China's exports to Sri Lanka reached USD 4.97 billion in 2024 — an estimated series from UN Comtrade, flagged as estimated rather than a directly reported figure. Because the value is an estimate, confirm it against Sri Lanka's own official import statistics before relying on it for business planning. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (China customs, exporter reporting, 2024), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Sri Lanka?

There is no comprehensive China–Sri Lanka free-trade agreement in force, so Chinese-origin goods are generally assessed at the Most Favoured Nation (MFN) rate under Sri Lanka's customs tariff. Both countries are members of the Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement (APTA), which grants preferential rates on a limited set of agreed tariff lines. Exact rates depend on the HS code; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official Sri Lanka Customs tariff schedule.

What VAT and duties apply to imports into Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka's standard VAT rate is 18%. Imported goods are also subject to applicable customs duty and any additional levies depending on the HS code and the current fiscal policy. Specific rates must be verified against Sri Lanka Customs / the Inland Revenue Department — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release.

What certification is required to import goods into Sri Lanka?

The Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI) is the national standards body; a defined list of products is subject to mandatory SLSI standards and conformity/certification requirements. Which specific conformity programme applies depends on the product category, so confirm the requirements for your goods with SLSI or your appointed inspection agency before shipping.

What does China export to Sri Lanka?

The bulk of China's exports to Sri Lanka is machinery and equipment, electronics and electrical goods, textiles and apparel, plastics and rubber products, and construction/building materials — an indicative profile consistent with Sri Lanka's import demand. The precise HS-code breakdown should be confirmed against official Sri Lankan customs statistics.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to Sri Lanka?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and any applicable SLSI conformity documentation. Goods arrive mainly by sea through the Port of Colombo, a major transshipment hub in South Asia, with direct services from Chinese ports. Typical sea freight from China is around 15–25 days depending on the origin and route, re-confirmed at order time. Source: general geographic/public infrastructure context; specific port throughput figures are data gap / not estimated.
