Importing from China to Brazil — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the live catalog snapshot of 2026-08-07 and can be verified on the original listings.

What are the steps to import from China to Brazil?

Four steps:

  • 1. Decide product & quantity — pick the items and target volume for your market.
  • 2. Find the factory supply directly from Chinese factories — our platform sources the listing at the factory-direct wholesale price.
  • 3. Inspect & confirm — goods are photo/video-confirmed before shipping and dispatched only after your confirmation.
  • 4. Ship to your door — small parcel or dedicated line, with tracking.

The service fee is charged only to the buyer: under ¥1K at 15% or a flat ¥150, whichever is higher; ¥1K–10K at 10%; over ¥10K at 8%.

How much does importing from China to Brazil cost?

Your landed cost has four parts: goods (factory wholesale price) + platform service fee + freight + import duties & taxes.

The service fee is transparent and tiered: under ¥1K at 15% or a flat ¥150, whichever is higher; ¥1K–10K at 10%; over ¥10K at 8% — charged only to the buyer.

Current catalog price bands (snapshot 2026-08-07): pet supplies ¥0.14–10.2/pc, car phone mounts ¥1.67–7.5/pc, LED strips ¥1.88–11.88/pc.

Are Chinese suppliers' minimum order quantities (MOQs) high?

No. Current catalog MOQs run 50–500 units depending on the category:

  • Pet supplies: 100–200 pcs
  • Car phone mounts: 100–500 pcs
  • LED strips: 50–100 rolls

Small trial orders are welcome, so you can validate the Brazilian market before scaling up.

How long does delivery from China to Brazil take?

Factory lead time is 5–25 days depending on the item. Small parcels arrive door-to-door in 10–20 days with tracking; shipments over 50 kg move on dedicated sea or air lines at a lower per-kg cost.

How do I avoid being scammed by Chinese suppliers?

Our platform handles it:

  • Goods are photo/video-confirmed before shipping and dispatched only after your confirmation.
  • Transit damage is covered by shipping insurance.
  • Quality issues are handled within 15 days with photos/videos (return, replacement or re-shipment per responsibility).
  • Not satisfied with your first order? The service fee is refundable.
How are taxes and duties calculated for Brazil?

Low-value parcels to Brazil fall under the Remessa Conforme simplified taxation scheme for small shipments.

Exact rates depend on the HS code of each item and each shipment. Check the Receita Federal (Brazilian Federal Revenue) portal with the HS code for authoritative figures before ordering — we also include duties and taxes in your full quote.

Can small businesses or individuals import from China?

Yes. Small trial orders under ¥1K are supported. We verify the real importing entity before providing the service, so individuals and small businesses can import safely and compliantly.
