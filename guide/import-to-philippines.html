Importing from China to the Philippines — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-21, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

How large is China–Philippines trade, and who is the Philippines' biggest trading partner?

Bilateral trade reached USD 72.29 billion in 2025 (China exports USD 55.58B; imports USD 16.71B from the Philippines). China has been the Philippines' largest trading partner and top import source for 10 consecutive years. Source: China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, updated 2026-04.

What is the Philippines' No.1 import category?

Electrical and electronic equipment — USD 33.55 billion in 2025, followed by mineral fuels (USD 18.07B) and machinery (USD 11.83B). Source: UN COMTRADE via TradingEconomics, 2025.

How dependent is the Philippines on Chinese imports?

China supplied 31.7% of Philippine imports in June 2026 (USD 4.35B that month); H1 2026 total imports hit USD 77.5B, up 17% YoY. In 2025, imports from China totaled USD 40.27B out of USD 141.85B. Source: TradingEconomics, June 2026 / COMTRADE 2025.

What certification do electronics need to enter the Philippine market?

Products on the BPS mandatory list (updated yearly) need a PS mark (factory inspection, 3-year validity) or ICC mark (no factory inspection, per-batch). Energy products need an energy label (1-year validity). Electrical supply: 230V/60Hz. Certification takes about 10–12 weeks; CB reports with national differences are accepted. Source: MofCom "Philippines Market Access & Conformity Assessment Requirements" PDF, extracted 2026-08-21.

When did RCEP take effect for the Philippines?

June 2, 2023 (GACC announcement of May 24, 2023). The ACFTA 3.0 upgrade protocol was signed in Kuala Lumpur on Oct 28, 2025, adding digital economy, green economy and supply-chain connectivity, with priority on electronics standards cooperation — it is not yet in force. Source: GACC 2023 / MofCom Department of International Trade 2025.

How digitally mature is the Philippine market for e-commerce content?

97.5M internet users (83.8% penetration), 90.8M social media users (78.0%), 62.3M TikTok users, median age 26.1, urbanization 48.8% — a young, highly connected market ideal for short-video commerce. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025: Philippines, January 2025.
