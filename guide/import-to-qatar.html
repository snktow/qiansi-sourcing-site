Importing from China to Qatar — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Qatar trade?

China's exports to Qatar reached about USD 296 million (ESTIMATED) in 2024 (296,368,097, China-side, 6-digit leaf rows only). This is a HS-6 leaf / partial figure: the UN Comtrade public preview API caps each response at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate-level rows with 6-digit leaf rows, so a full row-sum of USD 898 million double-counts across levels and is not the true total. The leaf figure is indicative/partial — NOT an authoritative total. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, period=2024, flowCode=X, partnerCode=634), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Qatar?

Qatar has been a WTO member since 13 January 1996 (GATT since 7 April 1994) and applies the GCC common external tariff of 5% ad valorem on the CIF value of most imported products, per GCC Law No. 41/2002 and the GCC Unified Customs Law in force since 1 January 2003. Product gradients include urea 30%, records/musical instruments 15%, and about 100% on pork, alcohol and tobacco. There is no US–Qatar free-trade agreement or BIT; other bilateral FTAs beyond the GCC are a data gap. Exact duty rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time. Sources: WTO Member Information – Qatar, US ITA trade.gov Country Commercial Guide (Import Tariffs / Trade Agreements pages, last published 2025-08-20), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Qatar?

Qatar has not introduced a value-added tax (VAT) as of this snapshot — the standard VAT rate is reported as Not Applicable (NA) by PwC Tax Summaries for Qatar. So general imported goods are not subject to a national VAT at this time. TradingEconomics does not list Qatar in its sales-tax-rate series (that side is a data gap and was not used as the basis). Re-confirm the latest fiscal status with the Qatari authorities before import. Source: PwC Tax Summaries – Qatar, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Qatar?

The GCC Standardization Organization (GSO) sets regional standards and conformity assessment, and the Gulf Conformity Mark (G-Mark) is the regional conformity mark (gso.org.sa). For meat and certain food imports, a health certificate issued by the country of export plus a 'Halal' slaughter certificate from an approved Islamic center are required. A finer, product-by-product mandatory standards list could not be verified because Qatar's Ministry of Public Health site is a JS single-page app (data gap); rely on the GS/GCC framework plus the Halal/health requirement as the operative baseline. Confirm category-specific requirements with the competent authority before import. Sources: GSO (gso.org.sa), US ITA trade.gov (Import Requirements and Documentation), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does Qatar import from China?

Qatar's main imports from China (indicative, incomplete, per the top 500-row preview, 6-digit leaf rows only) are led by petroleum oils (HS271019, ~USD 140 million), followed by silicon (HS280469, ~USD 46 million), garlic (HS070320, ~USD 12.4 million), asphalt (HS270810, ~USD 12.3 million) and ginger (HS091011, ~USD 7.1 million). These are partial top-500 results and NOT a complete picture. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports for imports from China to Qatar?

Qatar's main gateways are Hamad Port (the principal commercial and container port), Doha Port (the older harbour in the city, now focused on cruise and urban functions) and Al Ruwais Port on the north-west coast, all listed by Mwani Qatar (Qatar Ports Management Company). The Mesaieed industrial/energy zone is widely referenced but is not listed among the Mwani-managed commercial ports (Mwani Qatar lists Hamad, Doha and Al Ruwais), being served by a separate operator. Port names are stable geographic/logistics facts. Source: Mwani Qatar (mwani.com.qa), retrieved 2026-08-23; Mesaieed not in Mwani's managed-port list.
