Importing from China to Georgia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked as a data gap.

How large is China–Georgia trade?

A China-reporting-side export figure for 2024 is not available from the public UN Comtrade preview — this is a data gap, not an estimate. The China–Georgia Free Trade Agreement entered into force on 2018-01-01 (established background), which makes Georgia the one country in this guide group that has a dedicated FTA with China. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (China customs); China–Georgia FTA background; retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariff advantage does the China–Georgia Free Trade Agreement provide?

The China–Georgia FTA, in force since 2018-01-01 (established background), provides preferential tariff treatment on agreed product lines between the two countries — a genuine import-cost advantage relative to MFN rates for covered goods. Eligibility generally requires a certificate of origin. The precise coverage depends on the HS code, so confirm the latest agreed schedule for your goods against the official FTA lists and treat any figure as indicative pending the latest official release.

What VAT applies to imports into Georgia?

Georgia's standard value-added tax rate is 18%, applied at customs on imported goods. Reduced or exempt treatment can apply to certain categories — verify against Georgia's Revenue Service and confirm the latest official release. Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Georgia, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What trade blocs and arrangements apply to Georgia?

Georgia is not a member of the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) and has a Deep and Comprehensive Free Trade Area (DCFTA) with the European Union, alongside the China–Georgia FTA. Since it is not an EAEU member, the EAEU Common External Tariff does not apply to Georgia's imports. Source: eaeunion.org membership list; EU trade policy on Georgia; retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Georgia?

The Georgian national standards body is GEOSTM. Specific voluntary or mandatory conformity requirements depend on the product category — this is a data gap here (the GEOSTM portal was not reachable at the time of writing), so confirm applicability for your goods with the Georgian authorities. Treat any figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to Georgia?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or consignment note, and — to claim China–Georgia FTA preference — a certificate of origin. Georgia's principal import-export gateways are the Black Sea ports of Batumi and Poti, which also serve as the Caspian–Europe corridor onward from Central Asia. Exact routes (sea, or rail and road across the Caucasus) and transit times depend on the origin and are re-confirmed at order time. Source: general geographic / transport context.
