Importing from China to South Africa — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

How large is China–South Africa trade?

China's exports to South Africa reached USD 21.81 billion in 2024. The two countries also differ in their reporting methods, so the China-side export figure is used as the authoritative number. This is an estimated series from UN Comtrade (not a directly reported figure); Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so any per-row sum is only a partial subset, NOT the total — the headline above is the cmdCode=TOTAL estimate. Confirm against the destination country's official import statistics before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (China customs, reporterCode=156, period=2024, flowCode=X, cmdCode=TOTAL), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into South Africa?

South Africa is a member of the Southern African Customs Union (SACU) (together with Botswana, Namibia, Lesotho and Eswatini), so a common external tariff applies to goods entering the union from outside. China is one of South Africa's largest trading partners, but there is no general China–South Africa free-trade agreement removing duties, so the standard SACU tariff schedule applies. Exact rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release.

What VAT applies to imports into South Africa?

South Africa's standard VAT rate is 15%, applied at customs on imported goods. A reduced or zero rate can apply to certain categories (e.g. basic foodstuffs). Specific rates must be verified against the South African Revenue Service (SARS) — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release. Source: public tax-rate reference (TradingEconomics), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into South Africa?

Many imported products into South Africa must meet the standards of the South African Bureau of Standards (SABS). Some regulated goods require a compulsory SABS/NRCS certificate of conformity or a South African standard (e.g. for electrical products, certain electronics and safety-critical items) before they may be sold. Certification requirements are category-specific and should be confirmed for your product.

What does China export to South Africa?

South Africa imports from China a large share of machinery and equipment, electronics and components, vehicles and parts, renewable-energy equipment (solar panels, batteries), construction materials, textiles, and consumer goods. Source: UN Comtrade / COMTRADE export composition (China customs), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What documentation and shipping routes are used for imports from China to South Africa?

Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and a certificate of origin, plus a customs clearance declaration to SARS. Entry is mainly by sea through the Port of Durban (the busiest container port in sub-Saharan Africa) or the Port of Cape Town, with inland distribution to Johannesburg and elsewhere. Exact transit times depend on the origin and route and are re-confirmed at order time.
