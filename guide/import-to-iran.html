Importing from China to Iran — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Iran trade?

China's exports to Iran reached USD 1.33 billion (ESTIMATED) in 2024 (1,326,499,769, China-side FOB). This is a raw partial/lower-bound figure: the UN Comtrade public preview API caps each response at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate-level with 6-digit leaf rows, so a simple row-sum double-counts and understates the true total. Treat it as indicative/partial, NOT an authoritative total. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, period=2024, flowCode=X, partnerCode=364), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Iran?

Iran is a WTO observer in negotiating membership (not a full member), sits under a comprehensive US sanctions framework (OFAC Iran Sanctions), and the EAEU has confirmed it has agreed to launch free-trade-agreement talks with Iran — but no signing/effective date is confirmed yet. Exact duty rates depend on the HS code and are confirmed at order time; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official release. Sources: WTO, OFAC, EAEU, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Iran?

Iran's value-added / sales tax standard rate is 10%, sourced from the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Finance (reported via Trading Economics). Reduced rates can apply to specific categories. Verify the current rate with the relevant Iranian tax authority before import — treat any single figure as indicative only. Source: Trading Economics Iran sales-tax rate, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Iran?

Data gap: Iran's official standards and import-authority sites (ISIRI isiri.org, standard.ir, imts.ir) were all unreachable this session, so mandatory certification/conformity requirements could not be verified from an official primary source. As background only (not official verification), Iranian imports generally must meet Iranian National Standards (ISIRI) and certain regulated goods require a Certificate of Conformity (COC) plus an import licence. Confirm category-specific requirements with the competent authority before import.

What does Iran import from China?

Iran's main imports from China (indicative, incomplete, per the 500-row preview) include oilseeds and oleaginous fruits (HS12 chapter, ~USD 193 million), organic chemicals (HS29, ~USD 179 million), sunflower seeds (HS1206, ~USD 131 million), further oilseed sub-items (HS1212) and inorganic chemicals (HS28). CAUTION: these rows mix chapter-level with 6-digit levels, so they double-count and are indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Iran?

Iran's main gateways are Bandar Abbas (largest seaport) and Bandar-e Emam Khomeyni on the Persian Gulf, plus Astara (Iran–Azerbaijan border, rail/road node) and Bandar Anzali on the Caspian Sea. The International North–South Transport Corridor (INSTC) and the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route link the Caspian to the Persian Gulf; land crossings include Bazargan (Turkey), Sarakhs (Turkmenistan), Mirjaveh (Pakistan) and Qasr-e Shirin (Iraq). Port names are stable geographic/logistics facts; Iran's port-authority sites were unreachable this session, so the corridor framework is corroborated by the EAEU official page. Sources: EAEU, retrieved 2026-08-23; port authority = Data gap.
