Importing from China to Algeria — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are explicitly marked.

How large is China–Algeria trade?

China's exports to Algeria reached an estimated USD 11.68B in 2024 (11,682,167,683, China-side FOB). UN Comtrade flags this TOTAL aggregate as ESTIMATED (legacyEstimationFlag=4, isReported=false). Data caliber: the free preview caps per-commodity detail at 500 rows and mixes HS aggregate with 6-digit leaf rows, so a leaf-only sum (~USD 393.2M) or full row-sum (~USD 1.25B) is only a partial subset, NOT the total. Confirm against official China/Algeria statistics before quoting. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (reporterCode=156, partnerCode=12, cmdCode=TOTAL), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Algeria?

Algeria applies its own national customs tariff (duties are set nationally), NOT the EU Common Customs Tariff or a shared bloc tariff. WTO accession under negotiation (not yet a member); applies its own national customs tariff. There is no Algeria–China comprehensive free trade agreement in force, so Chinese goods generally pay the standard MFN duty under Algeria's national tariff schedule. Exact rates depend on the HS code — confirm against the latest algeria customs tariff. Sources: Algeria customs authority; WTO (trade status), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What VAT applies to imports into Algeria?

Algeria's standard value-added tax (VAT) rate is 19%. Confirmed by TradingEconomics (Algeria Sales Tax Rate = 19%). Reduced rates/specific categories were not independently sourced this session (Data gap) — confirm with the latest algeria tax authority / PwC release. Source: TradingEconomics (Algeria Sales Tax Rate, sourced to Algerian General Directorate of Taxes (DGI)), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What certification is required to import goods into Algeria?

Algeria is not an EU candidate, so EU CE marking does not apply here; products must instead meet Algeria's national conformity / technical-requirements regime (Algerian Institute of Standardization (IANOR)). Importer registration and customs declaration follow algeria customs. The specific regulated-product list was not independently sourced this session (Data gap) — confirm with Algerian Institute of Standardization (IANOR) / algeria customs. Sources: Algerian Institute of Standardization (IANOR); Algeria customs authority, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does Algeria import from China?

Algeria's main imports from China (indicative and incomplete, per the 500-row free preview) include HSHS160414 prepared tuna, skipjack and bonito (~USD 102.46M), HSHS090220 green tea, not fermented (~USD 42.63M) and HSHS210210 active yeasts (~USD 31.80M). CAUTION: these are a truncated, mixed-level preview subset and very likely NOT the true top categories — indicative only. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What are the main ports and land routes for imports from China to Algeria?

The Port of Algiers is Algeria's principal cargo gateway, and plus Port of Oran & Port of Annaba, operated by Algerian Ports (ATP) / Port of Algiers. Port names and operator identities are stable logistics facts sourced from the port authorities. Measured throughput/TEU figures were not reliably sourced this session (Data gap). Sources: Algerian Ports (ATP) / Port of Algiers, retrieved 2026-08-23.
