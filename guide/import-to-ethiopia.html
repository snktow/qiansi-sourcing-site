Importing from China to Ethiopia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "data gap / not estimated" or flagged as estimates.

How large is China–Ethiopia trade?

China's exports to Ethiopia reached USD 3.14 billion in 2024 — an estimated series from UN Comtrade, flagged as estimated rather than a directly reported figure. Because the value is an estimate, confirm it against Ethiopia's own official import statistics before relying on it for business planning. Source: UN Comtrade public preview API (China customs, exporter reporting, 2024), retrieved 2026-08-23.

What tariffs apply to imports from China into Ethiopia?

Ethiopia is a COMESA member (and is pursuing WTO accession). There is no China–Ethiopia free-trade agreement, so Chinese-origin goods are assessed at the Most Favoured Nation (MFN) rate under Ethiopia's national tariff schedule. Exact rates depend on the HS code and the current trade regime; treat any single figure as indicative and confirm the latest official tariff.

What VAT and duties apply to imports into Ethiopia?

Ethiopia's standard VAT rate is 15%. Customs duty depends on the HS code and any applicable exemptions. Specific rates must be verified against the Ethiopian Customs Commission schedule — treat any single figure as indicative only and confirm the latest official release.

What certification is required to import goods into Ethiopia?

Ethiopia applies Mandatory Ethiopian National Standards (as notified by the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration) and conformity/certification requirements for imported goods. Bodies commonly cited include the Ethiopian Standards Agency (ESA) and the Ethiopian Conformity Assessment Enterprise (ECAE), but the exact current designation could not be independently verified from a live source at the time of writing (data gap) — requirements are category-specific and should be confirmed with the competent authority before ordering. Source: Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (Ethiopia), motri.gov.et, retrieved 2026-08-23.

What does China export to Ethiopia?

The bulk of China's exports to Ethiopia is machinery and equipment, electronics and electrical goods, construction and building materials, textiles and vehicles/parts — an indicative profile consistent with China's exports across African markets. The precise HS-code breakdown should be confirmed against official Ethiopian customs statistics.

How do goods ship to a landlocked country like Ethiopia?

Ethiopia is landlocked, so most seaborne imports arrive via the Port of Djibouti and travel inland on the Addis Ababa–Djibouti Railway (Ethio-Djibouti Standard Gauge), linking the port to the capital. Bole International Airport in Addis Ababa handles air freight for smaller or urgent consignments. Standard import documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin and any applicable conformity documentation. Exact transit times depend on the origin and route and are re-confirmed at order time. Source: general geographic/public infrastructure context; specific rail/port and air-freight capacity are data gap / not estimated.
