Importing from China to Malaysia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-23, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

How big is China–Malaysia trade?

Bilateral trade reached USD 191.67 billion in 2025, with China exporting USD 103.68 billion to Malaysia. China has been Malaysia's largest trading partner for 17 consecutive years. Source: China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs country profile of Malaysia, updated 2026-04.

What is Malaysia's largest import category?

Electrical & electronic products, accounting for 29.4% of total imports; followed by chemicals (9.5%), petroleum products (9.3%) and machinery (8.7%). Source: TradingEconomics / Department of Statistics Malaysia, updated 2026-08.

How much does Malaysia import from China?

USD 82.40 billion in 2025, about 24.3% of Malaysia's total imports of USD 339.52 billion — its #1 source country. Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2025.

What tax applies to low-value imported goods?

A 10% sales tax on Low Value Goods priced at or below RM500 (≈USD 124), effective 1 January 2024, charged by customs-registered sellers; the CIF ≤ RM500 air-courier de minimis exemption from import duty still applies. Source: Royal Malaysian Customs Department LVG FAQ (version 2023-11-06), fetched 2026-08-23.

How digital is Malaysia's consumer market?

97.7% internet penetration (34.9M users) and 25.1M social media users (70.2% of population); parcels delivered per capita jumped 42% in 2024 to 37 per person, total parcels +43.7% to 1.263 billion. Source: DataReportal Digital 2025 (January 2025); MCMC Postal & Courier Services Industry Performance Report 2024 via the Economic & Commercial Office of the Chinese Embassy in Malaysia (2025-11-03).

What certifications do electronic products need in Malaysia?

SIRIM QAS is Malaysia's national certification body offering product certification, testing and energy-efficiency schemes; electrical/electronic goods are subject to regulatory approval per product category (the mandatory list is to be confirmed per HS code), and all LVG imports still follow standard import procedures with permits for controlled goods. Source: SIRIM QAS official site (fetched 2026-08-23); Royal Malaysian Customs Department LVG FAQ.
