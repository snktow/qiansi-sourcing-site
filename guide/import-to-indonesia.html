Importing from China to Indonesia — Questions & Answers

Every figure below is from the market data snapshot of 2026-08-21, with the source named in each answer. Items not yet verified are marked "to be verified".

Is China Indonesia's biggest trading partner?

Yes. In 2025, China–Indonesia bilateral trade reached USD 167.4 billion, up 13.4% year-on-year; China's exports to Indonesia were USD 85.3 billion (+11.3%). China is also Indonesia's largest foreign-investment source (including Hong Kong), and Indonesia is China's second-largest investment destination in ASEAN. Source: China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs country profile of Indonesia, updated 2026-04.

How much of Indonesia's imports come from China?

In 2025 Indonesia imported USD 87.54 billion from China — 36.2% of its total imports, making China the largest import source (Singapore USD 19.17 billion and Japan USD 14.47 billion follow). Source: TradingEconomics / UN COMTRADE 2025.

Is Indonesia's import demand still growing?

Yes, strongly. Indonesia's imports reached USD 137.24 billion in the first half of 2026, up 18.69% year-on-year, and the June 2026 monthly figure set a record at USD 25.91 billion (+32.27%). Source: TradingEconomics, August 2026 update.

What does Indonesia import most?

Machinery and equipment are Indonesia's largest import category at about 19% of total imports (2025), followed by oil and gas (about 17%), steel (5.4%), organic chemicals (4.8%) and vehicles (4.5%). Source: TradingEconomics, overview figures.

How big and how young is Indonesia's market?

Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest population — 284.4 million (2025) — and one of its youngest, with a median age of 30.4, internet penetration of 74.6% (212 million users) and urbanization of 59.5%. Sources: TradingEconomics (2025); DataReportal Digital 2025: Indonesia (January 2025).

What cooperation mechanisms exist between China and Indonesia?

China's Belt and Road Initiative is docked with Indonesia's Global Maritime Fulcrum framework: the MOU was signed in October 2018 and renewed in July 2022, underpinning the Two Countries, Twin Parks scheme, the Regional Comprehensive Economic Corridor and the Jakarta–Bandung High-Speed Railway (opened October 2023). Bilateral cooperation explicitly covers the digital economy and e-commerce. Source: China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs country profile of Indonesia, updated 2026-04.
