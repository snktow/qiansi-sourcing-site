Trend conclusion: China's HS 9405 exports total about US$42.2B/yr (2024), flat from 2023 but down from a 2021 peak (US$46.6B). The structural opportunity is not in total volume — it's a shift from declining Western markets (UK, Canada, Japan, Australia) to emerging Gulf, ASEAN and LatAm demand (UAE, Vietnam, Mexico).

Data-source note (honest limits): The free UN Comtrade preview API we use cannot return US (840) or India (356) as destination markets — these two are typically the largest buyers in full data, so they are intentionally absent from the sample above. Competitor export values are also only partially reliable (several countries return anomalous or missing values in the free preview).

No fabricated numbers: every figure above carries its source URL and query time. Where data is unavailable or low-confidence we say so, rather than guess. A full, reliable picture (including US / India and solid competitor quantification) needs a paid Comtrade key — ask us and we'll scope it.
